About this project

  1. Cancer cell dataset

    – cell samples, each labelled malignant (M) or benign (B), with nucleus measurements.

  2. Train the models

    – models learn from radius and texture, then are saved as .pkl files.

  3. Flask API

    This page sends the two measurements to /predict; the backend loads the chosen model and answers.

  4. Dashboard

    Results, charts and tables here are drawn from the API, never typed in by hand.

What the measurements mean

Radius mean
Average distance from the centre of the nucleus to its edge. Larger nuclei are more often malignant.
Texture mean
Variation of gray-scale values inside the nucleus. More uneven texture is more often malignant.

How to read the charts

  • Accuracy: share of all test samples classified correctly.
  • Precision (M): of the cells called malignant, how many truly were.
  • Recall (M): of the truly malignant cells, how many were found.
  • AUC: how well a model ranks malignant above benign (1.0 perfect, 0.5 coin flip).

Limits to keep in mind

  • The models use only two of the dataset's measurements, so some malignant and benign cells look alike to them.
  • There are no microscope images in this dataset, only numbers measured from them, so the charts plot measurements rather than pictures.
  • Accuracy comes from one 80/20 split of – samples; a different split would give slightly different numbers.
  • This is a college project. It is not a medical diagnosis and no substitute for a qualified healthcare provider.
